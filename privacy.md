The Agent Mesh Communication Protocol (AMCP) ("we", "us", "our", or "Company") operates the https://agentmeshcommunicationprotocol.github.io website (the "Website"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise handle your information when you visit our Website.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Contact Information**: Name, email address, phone number (when you contact us)
- **Account Information**: Username, password, profile information (if applicable)
- **Communication**: Messages, feedback, and inquiries you send us
- **Survey Responses**: Responses to surveys or questionnaires

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage Data**: Pages visited, time spent, links clicked
- **Device Information**: Browser type, operating system, device type
- **IP Address**: Your IP address and approximate location
- **Cookies**: Session cookies and tracking cookies

### 2.3 Third-Party Information

- **GitHub**: If you authenticate via GitHub
- **Social Media**: If you share content on social platforms
- **Analytics Providers**: Data from Google Analytics

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for:

- **Service Delivery**: Providing and improving the Website
- **Communication**: Responding to your inquiries and requests
- **Analytics**: Understanding how users interact with the Website
- **Marketing**: Sending newsletters and promotional content (with consent)
- **Security**: Detecting and preventing fraud and abuse
- **Legal Compliance**: Meeting legal and regulatory requirements

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your information for:

- **Account Data**: As long as your account is active
