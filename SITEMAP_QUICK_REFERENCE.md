### Step-by-Step Submission

1. **Go to Google Search Console**
   - URL: https://search.google.com/search-console

2. **Select Your Property**
   - Choose: `https://agentmeshcommunicationprotocol.github.io`

3. **Click "Sitemaps" (Left Menu)**
   - Under "Index" section

4. **Click "Add a new sitemap"**
   - Button at top right

5. **Paste Sitemap URL**
   ```
   https://agentmeshcommunicationprotocol.github.io/sitemap.xml
   ```

6. **Click "Submit"**
   - Wait for confirmation

7. **Verify Success**
   - Status should show "Success" (green checkmark)
   - Pages discovered count will appear
