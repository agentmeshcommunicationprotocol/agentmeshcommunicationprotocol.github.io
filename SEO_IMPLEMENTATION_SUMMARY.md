## Metrics to Track

### Search Performance Metrics
1. **Impressions**: Total times your page appears in search results
2. **Clicks**: Total clicks from search results
3. **CTR**: Click-through rate (clicks / impressions)
4. **Average Position**: Average ranking position in search results

### Traffic Metrics
1. **Organic Traffic**: Visitors from organic search
2. **Traffic by Keyword**: Traffic for each target keyword
3. **Traffic Growth**: Week-over-week or month-over-month growth

### Engagement Metrics
1. **Time on Page**: Average time spent on page
2. **Bounce Rate**: Percentage of single-page sessions
3. **Pages per Session**: Average pages viewed per session
4. **Conversion Rate**: Percentage of visitors who convert

### Ranking Metrics
1. **Keyword Rankings**: Position for each target keyword
2. **Ranking Changes**: Week-over-week ranking changes
3. **New Rankings**: New keywords entering top 100
4. **Lost Rankings**: Keywords dropping out of top 100
